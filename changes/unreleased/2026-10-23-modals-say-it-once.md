- **UI: Modals say each thing once**
  - Decision, attack and thwart target, combat boost, payment, order, card stats, reset stats and Wakanda Forever modals no longer repeat a card, a value or a title. The header trigger is dropped when the body already shows the card.
  - Action menus (identity, ally, tableau) write a row's verb and value once. The Wakanda recap list, the generic kickers and the repeated Game/All labels are gone.
  - The infinite-loop modal shows the cards of the loop side by side. Option descriptions no longer show raw LaTeX.
  - The type and HP line under a card thumbnail in the Distribute modal no longer touches the thumbnail (Dossier showed it most).

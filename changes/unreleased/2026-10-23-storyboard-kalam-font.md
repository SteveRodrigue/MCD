type: Changed
---

- **UI: Every theme has its own font: Pop-Art Lilita One, Storyboard Panels Kalam, Splash Page Boogaloo, Golden Age Newsprint Merriweather, Night Edition JetBrains Mono, Hero HUD Lexend, S.H.I.E.L.D. Dossier Special Elite**
  - `font-comic`, `font-body`, `font-serif` and `font-mono` now follow the `--font-comic`, `--font-body`, `--font-serif` and `--font-mono` variables, and a theme can override them (`fonts` in the theme tokens). Pop-Art sets Lilita One for headlines, Storyboard Panels sets Kalam, Splash Page sets Boogaloo, Golden Age Newsprint sets Merriweather and Night Edition sets JetBrains Mono and Hero HUD sets Lexend and the Dossier sets Special Elite, bundled with `@fontsource`.
  - A theme has a default Headlines + Body pair, and the Newspaper (Daily Bugle) can override either of them (`fonts.newspaper`). Digits in the default Bangers stack now use the body font (Inter Bold) instead of Comic Relief. See `docs/templates/modals/FONT_RECORD.md`.
  - Theme fonts: Headlines and Body are both required for every theme; the Serif slot is gone (the newspaper typeface is the Newspaper pair); the Mono slot is now "Numbers & codes" (`fonts.numbers`, `--font-numbers`).
  - The theme fonts table of `docs/templates/modals/FONT_RECORD.md` is generated from the theme tokens (`npm run docs:fonts`, run by the pre-commit hook) and a test fails when it is stale.
  - Guard tests (`tests/ui/font-guards.test.ts`): theme code names only the font roles, the lab font list, stylesheet and files stay in step, and the font variables, Tailwind and the CSS defaults agree. The Pop-Art form field now reads the Body role instead of `font-sans`.
  - Lab font test: each font list has a `-` and `+` at its right to change the size of that text in 1px steps (headlines, body, newspaper headlines, newspaper body, numbers and codes). It is added to the size the game gives the text, is remembered with the fonts, and is cleared by the "Theme fonts" button.
  - Lab font test: a "Save font choices" button writes the fonts and size changes chosen for the open style to `logs/lab/font-choices.json` (dev server only, git-ignored), one entry per style, so they can be read later when the themes are updated.
  - Numbers and codes use Nunito in every theme (Night, Dossier and Hero HUD no longer use their own font for them), and each theme now has the text size change chosen in the lab: Body +1 (Pop-Art, Newsprint, Night, Hero HUD), +2 (Storyboard, Dossier), +3 (Splash); Numbers +1 everywhere; Splash Newspaper body +4; Dossier Newspaper headlines -1. Small text is sized with `text-nano`, `text-micro` and `text-mini` (9, 10 and 11px), which add the change; card faces keep their sizes.
  - The design lab (`/templates`) and its 36 lab-only fonts are built only for the dev server: the production build is about 1,050 kB smaller (3,344 kB to 2,294 kB of assets) and a `/templates` address opens the game. A test builds for production and fails if a lab chunk or lab font ships.
  - The game screens (setup, mulligan, game) now set the theme's font roles and size changes on their root, and unlabelled text is the theme's Body font instead of the system sans. The card editor and the lab are not themed, and a test keeps the editor out.
  - Pop-Art's Body font is Lilita One, the same as its headlines (Inter was compared in the lab and rejected for small text). The intro banner ("MISSION CONTROL", "MARVEL CHAMPIONS", the subtitle) is pinned to Bangers so it keeps its look in every theme.
  - The theme choice on the first screen is always open (it was a collapsed panel): the 7 themes and Random each game can be chosen before a game starts, and the first screen's fonts follow the choice at once.
  - The Daily Bugle action sheet, the end-turn flash, the top bar Bugle button and the EXTRA! button read the theme's Newspaper fonts and text sizes instead of a fixed serif.
  - The combat log follows the theme: the font selector (Font: button), its presets and the Komika font files are removed, the dialogue uses the theme's Body font and the log uses the theme's text sizes. Modals, drawers and newspapers (everything rendered through the modal layer) now carry the theme's fonts and sizes, including the Daily Bugle and the end-turn flash.
  - Small text in the themed modals now uses the Body font role instead of the Headlines role (no visible change today, since every theme uses one font for both), and the Daily Bugle sheet and end-turn flash use the Newspaper headline role for their accents. A test fails when small text uses the Headlines font in those files.
  - The modal frames use the Body font and headline-size text (titles, counters, burst words) names the Headlines font, so Headlines and Body are separate roles in the themed modals. No visible change today. A test fails when headline-size text in the theme layer, the kits and the modals names no font role.
  - The setup and mulligan screens use the Body font for small text and the size tokens for their tiny labels: the tiny text on the first screen is 1 to 3px bigger depending on the theme, following the theme's text size change. The Bangers banner is unchanged.
  - The hero and villain zones use the Body font for small text and the size tokens for their tiny labels (the smallest 8px labels become 9px), so the zones follow the theme's text size change.
  - The hand tray, top bar, villain phase stepper, combat log drawer and damage splash use the Body font for small text and the size tokens for their tiny labels.
  - The card components (card view, context menu, face-down encounter card, attachment fan) use the Body font for small text and the size tokens for their tiny labels; card faces keep their own sizes.
  - The font role rules (small text is Body, headline-size text names Headlines, no fixed tiny sizes) are enforced by tests over the whole themed UI, so new screens follow the theme's fonts and text sizes from the start.
  - The villain zone follows the theme: its panel, title, discard and deck slots, villain, scheme and side scheme panels and count pills take their frame from the selected theme (colours that carry a game meaning, such as threat, status and the stat boxes, stay). A new `badge` token gives every theme the shape of its small badges, and a test keeps literal comic looks out of the migrated screens.
  - The hero zone follows the theme: panel, ribbon, threat zone, identity and ally panels, tableau, slots and buttons take their frame from the selected theme; stat colours, statuses and action colours (attack red, thwart blue) keep their meaning. Stat boxes and action buttons are now shared components.
  - The hand tray and the out-of-play box follow the theme: panels, ribbon, pile sidebar, discard and nemesis buttons, End Turn and the empty-hand slot take their frame from the selected theme.
  - The top bar, the villain phase stepper, the scroll arrows, the card context menu and the damage splash follow the theme. The card inspect window is now a themed modal, and the damage splash uses each theme's sound-effect look (star burst or panel).
  - The first screen and the mulligan screen follow the theme: the comic book spread, the encounter and difficulty panels, the hero seats, the theme picker panel, the launch bars and the buttons take their frame from the selected theme, while the Bangers MARVEL CHAMPIONS banner keeps its look; difficulty, status and action colours keep their meaning.
  - The page behind the game follows the theme: its background, texture and text colour come from the selected theme on the setup, mulligan and game screens (the card editor keeps the comic paper), and the loading screens use the theme's headline font.

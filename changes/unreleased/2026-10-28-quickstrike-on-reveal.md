- **Engine: a revealed Quickstrike minion now attacks the hero it engages**
  - The villain phase put a revealed minion into play without resolving Quickstrike, so only minions engaged by an action or a "put into play" effect attacked. Quickstrike is now one `resolveQuickstrike` step shared by all three entry paths, and resolves after the When Revealed abilities if the minion is still engaged (RR v1.8). Found by the new Quickstrike rule scenario.

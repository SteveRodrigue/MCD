- **Combat Log names the hero who acted, not the active player**
  - Immune, Tough, Piercing and damage-shield lines now carry the acting hero, so Black Panther's retaliation against an immune Madame Hydra no longer shows Iron Man (#515).
  - Villain-phase lines nobody caused (scheme threat, encounter cards dealt) no longer name the first player.
  - A player-phase line keeps the name of the player whose turn it was when it was written, instead of whoever is active when the log is viewed.

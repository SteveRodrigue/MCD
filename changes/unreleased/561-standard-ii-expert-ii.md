- **Feature (Data): Standard II and Expert II encounter sets ([Issue #561](https://github.com/SteveRodrigue/MCD/issues/561))**
  - Both sets are playable once The Hood pack is enabled, with no script: every ability uses shared effects.
  - Cards with the Setup keyword start in play, and "Standard Mode Only" and "Expert Mode Only" cards show the side of the mode.
  - New keyword Peril: while a player resolves such a card, other players cannot play cards or trigger abilities.

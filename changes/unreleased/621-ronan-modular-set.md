- **Content: The Ronan Modular Set (Kree Fanatic) is playable**
  - Turn the pack on in the Content screen, and Kree Fanatic appears in the modular slot at setup.
  - Ronan the Accuser, Judge, Jury, Executioner, The Accused, Bring the Hammer Down and You Dare Oppose Me? all follow their printed text.
  - New supplemental primitives: `CANNOT_HAVE_STATUS`, `ENGAGE_MINION`, the `IF_ACTIVATION_DEFEATED_CHARACTER` gate, `against` on `ACTIVATE_MINIONS`, `ANY_ENEMY` on `defeatedByAttackOf`, `ATTACHED` on `PUT_INTO_PLAY` and `DEAL_FACEDOWN` on `matchingDestination`.
- **Rules: A tie for "the hero with the fewest hit points" now asks the first player** (RR v1.8 First Player). This also applies to Mad Genius.
- **Rules: A boost card that puts itself into play as a side scheme is no longer also discarded.**

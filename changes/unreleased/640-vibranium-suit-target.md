- **Fix (Engine): Vibranium Suit lets the player choose the enemy, and Wakanda Forever! keeps the printed labels ([Issue #640](https://github.com/SteveRodrigue/MCD/issues/640))**
  - Vibranium Suit asks which enemy takes the moved damage, so it can hit an Ultron Drone. Before, the damage always went to the villain.
  - Minions that share a name in a target prompt show the hero they are engaged with.
  - With no damage on the hero, Vibranium Suit opens no prompt and the sequence continues.
  - Specials resolved by Wakanda Forever! now count as their printed "(attack)" or "(thwart)": Guard protects the villain, Stunned and Confused cancel them, Retaliate hits back and "after your hero attacks" Responses are offered.

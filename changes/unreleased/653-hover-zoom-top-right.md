- **Change (UI): Show the card hover zoom in the top right corner ([Issue #653](https://github.com/SteveRodrigue/MCD/issues/653))**
  - Every hover zoom is one upright copy pinned to the top right corner, above all UI, and clicks pass through it. Step banners no longer cut it.
  - Hovered cards keep their size, position and rotation, so hands and fans no longer wiggle. Unplayable cards stay gray and tagged while hovered.
  - The zoom shrinks to fit small windows. The Card Zooming setting still applies.

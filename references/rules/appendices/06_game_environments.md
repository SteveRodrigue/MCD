ENVIRONMENTS (BETA)ENVIRONMENTS (BETA)
When preparing for a game, a group of players may choose 
a game environment for that game, or they may decide to 
allow each player to choose their own environment for that 
game. The game environment establishes which Marvel 
Champions products can be used during player deck 
customization for that game. By choosing to use a smaller 
environment, players can test their creativity and skill by 
customizing their player decks using a smaller pool of player 
cards.
To play in a game environment, a player customizes their 
deck using only player cards included in the products 
designated by that environment. This includes the choice of 
which hero to play as.
A group may choose to play any scenario with any modular 
encounter sets, regardless of the chosen environment(s).
There are three types of environments:
CURRENT ENVIRONMENT
The Current Environment consists of the most recent 
products released for Marvel Champions , in addition to the 
core set.
The Current Environment includes the following products:
-  The Marvel Champions: The Card Game core set
-  Expansions
  The Age of Apocalypse  campaign expansion
  The Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.  campaign expansion
  The Civil War  custom scenario expansion
  The Fear No Evil campaign expansion 
(upon release)
-  Hero Packs
  Iceman  Hero Pack 
  Jubilee  Hero Pack 
  Nightcrawler  Hero Pack 
  Magneto  Hero Pack
  Black Panther  Hero Pack
  Silk Hero Pack 
  Falcon Hero Pack 
  Winter Soldier  Hero Pack 
  Wonder Man  Hero Pack 
  Hercules  Hero Pack 
-  All products not listed above that were released 
since the most recent version of this document was 
published (v1.8, Jul 2026 ).
LEGACY ENVIRONMENT
The Legacy Environment consists of all  Marvel Champions  
products. To play in the Legacy Environment, a player 
customizes their deck using player cards from any product 
in their collection.
LIMITED ENVIRONMENT
A limited environment allows a player who owns a number 
of older products to continue to play with those products 
while still reaping the benefits of playing within a smaller 
environment. To play in a Limited Environment, a player 
customizes their deck using cards from:
-  The Marvel Champions: The Card Game core set
-  Up to 3 campaign and/or custom scenario expansions
-  Up to 4 hero packs
While any expansion or hero pack may be chosen for a 
Limited Environment (and finding creative combinations 
of products from which to build could be an enjoyable 
experience for deckbuilding enthusiasts), a number 
of past Marvel Champions  products were developed 
together in waves. As a starting point for creating a Limited 
Environment, we recommend selecting three waves 
of related products to explore the synergies between 
those cards, and branching out to other, more creative 
combinations only after growing more familiar with the 
cardpool.

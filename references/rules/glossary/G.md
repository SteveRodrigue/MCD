### “GAINS”

If a card gains a characteristic (such as a trait, keyword, or
ability text) , the card functions as if it possesses the gained
characteristic. Gained characteristics are not considered to
be printed on the card.

### GAME ELEMENT

A game element is a component or person involved in
playing a game of Marvel Champions . All of the following
are game elements:
-  Cards
-  Decks
-  Discard Piles
-  Hands (of cards)
-  Hit Point Dials
-  Players
-  Tokens
*See also : * [Card Types](C.md#card-types), [Deck](D.md#deck), [Discard Pile](D.md#discard-pile), [Player](P.md#player), [Target](T.md#target)

### GUARD

While a minion with the guard keyword is engaged with a
player, that player cannot use cards they control to attack a
villain without this keyword.
-  The guard keyword is equivalent to the following
constant ability: “The engaged player cannot attack
any villain.”

### QUALIFIERS

If ability text includes a qualifier followed by multiple terms,
the qualifier applies to each item in the list, if applicable.
For example, in the phrase “each ready character and
attachment” the word “ready” applies to both “character”
and “attachment.”

### QUICKSTRIKE

After a minion with the quickstrike keyword engages a
player whose identity is in hero form, that minion attacks
that player.
-  The quickstrike keyword is equivalent to the following
triggered ability: “ Forced Response (Hero) : After this
minion engages a player, it attacks that player.”
-  If a minion with the quickstrike keyword is being
revealed, the quickstrike keyword resolves after
any “When Revealed ” abilities on that minion are
resolved.
